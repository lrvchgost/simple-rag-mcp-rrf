# Characters of the cycle

## Charles Swann
An aesthete and collector, a friend of the family. His hopeless love for Odette
is the plot of «Swann in Love» inside the first volume. He marries Odette and
is the father of Gilberte. Vinteuil's sonata with its «little phrase» becomes
the musical leitmotif of their romance.

## Odette de Crécy
A courtesan turned society woman, the «lady in pink» from the Botticelli
fresco. Idol of the Verdurins' «little clan». From a woman in love she slowly
becomes a social ruler; in the finale she is the Duchesse de Forcheville.

## Gilberte Swann
Daughter of Swann and Odette, the narrator's childhood love in Combray and
Paris. Later she marries Saint-Loup and enters the world of the Guermantes.

## Baron de Charlus
Palamède, brother of the Duc de Guermantes. Brilliant, venomous, passionate;
his fall is the «Sodom» of the cycle: in Doncières, Jupien's courtyard and
later he seeks humiliation and moments of power. One of the greatest characters
of the novel.

## Albertine Simonet
A young girl from the «little band» of Balbec. In the last volumes she is the
captive and the fugitive: the narrator keeps her in his apartment, torments
himself with jealousy (suspicion of her attachments to women), and she dies in
a fall from a horse.

## Duchess de Guermantes
Oriane, queen of the Parisian fireworks of wit. Her salon is the summit of the
«fav» world, open to the narrator only in the third volume.

## Françoise
The cook in Combray, later in the family's Paris apartment. Folk wisdom and
cruelty; Proust turns her worldview into a small anthropological study.

## Vinteuil
A composer, an outcast of the «little clan». His sonata and septet are the
cycle's only instance of «art as salvation», anticipating the narrator's final
plan for his own book.
